## 🎨 Current vision: "Energetic Sleek"
- High typographic contrast (The Power Scale: 11pt caps → 52pt bold).
- Physical Glass surfaces (no hairlines, whitespace grouping).
- Electric Cobalt (#245BFF) as the primary energy accent.
