**1. OVER-CLAIMING**
- "the iPhone app contains no analytics or advertising software of any kind": False. The Mapbox SDK ships with telemetry on by default, and its manifest declares Analytics as a purpose.
- "They differ in exactly one material way": False. The Mapbox SDK and its telemetry are iOS-only, and session handling also differs (keychain token vs Clerk cookies).
- "the web app uses product analytics" / "The web app uses PostHog": PostHog is uncommitted work-in-progress and not live. Present tense describes processing that doesn't happen yet.
- "Because the iPhone app contains no analytics and does no tracking, there is nothing to opt out of there": False. Mapbox analytics telemetry runs by default and nothing in the facts shows it disabled.
- "Anonymous visitors are not given a profile": Misleading. `identified_only` stops person profiles from being created but does not stop event capture or replay for anonymous visitors.
- "so property addresses, tenant names and lease figures are not captured in replays": Absolute claim the facts don't support. Text and attribute masking says nothing about images, canvas or map tiles, displayed photos, URLs or page titles.
- "so we can connect feedback to the account that gave it": UNVERIFIABLE FROM FACTS. The purpose of identification is not stated anywhere.
- "request timestamps, IP address, and rate-limiting counters … diagnose faults": UNVERIFIABLE FROM FACTS. No logging or rate limiting is established.
- "Mapbox | Map rendering and search": UNVERIFIABLE FROM FACTS. Search or geocoding is not established. If it exists, property addresses go to Mapbox.
- "the maps are centred on the properties you have entered": UNVERIFIABLE FROM FACTS.
- "Documents and photographs … deeds, leases, insurance certificates": UNVERIFIABLE FROM FACTS. The facts only say "documents".
- "short-lived, single-purpose upload ticket": The expiry of the presigned POST is UNVERIFIABLE FROM FACTS. Only the download URLs are stated to be short-lived.
- "Each is bound by contract to process data only on our instructions": UNVERIFIABLE FROM FACTS. It is also contradicted for Mapbox, which collects data for its own Analytics purpose, so it acts at least partly as a controller.
- "including in the United States, the European Union and Australia": UNVERIFIABLE FROM FACTS. No region is verified for S3, Neon, Vercel, Clerk or PostHog.
- "Your account … is deleted when you delete it": Deletion is not deployed. Also, the mirror row is anonymised and kept, not deleted.
- "remove the personal data held about you": Overstated. The user id is kept, membership rows are kept, and any personal data inside workspace records and S3 files stays.
- "kept in a de-identified form": Wrong term. A kept, linkable user id is pseudonymised and is still personal data under GDPR. "De-identified" is also a defined CCPA term with conditions that aren't shown to be met.
- "go to Account → Delete Account. This is immediate": The feature is not deployed and the UI path is UNVERIFIABLE FROM FACTS. Clean-up runs through an asynchronous webhook, so "immediate" is not established.
- "contact us and we will delete it" (Children): This conflicts with the stated policy "never hard-delete tenant data".
- "All traffic … uses HTTPS": UNVERIFIABLE FROM FACTS.
- "authentication and organisation boundaries are enforced by the API on every request": UNVERIFIABLE FROM FACTS.
- "We do not ask for your phone number, date of birth…": UNVERIFIABLE FROM FACTS. Clerk's configured sign-in fields are not established.
- "a property-management tool for property owners": UNVERIFIABLE FROM FACTS.

**2. UNDER-DISCLOSURE**
- Section 5 / section 6 bullets: The draft never says plainly that Mapbox telemetry in the iOS app is on by default and sends a user ID and precise location for Analytics. Section 5 mentions it, but other sections then contradict it.
- "Records you enter about your properties": Doesn't disclose that tenancy and ownership records contain personal data about third parties (tenants, co-owners) who are not users. It also doesn't say who the controller is for that data.
- (absent) Invitations: The accept-invitation flow means a workspace member submits an invitee's email before that person has any relationship with Valgate. This processing and its source are not disclosed.
- "If you delete your account, you lose your access": Doesn't disclose that a sole owner can delete their account and leave the workspace with no owner. It doesn't say who then controls those records or handles rights requests for them.
- (absent) Cookies: Clerk session cookies on the web are not mentioned at all. Neither is PostHog's cookie/localStorage use once it goes live.
- "Only signed-in users are identified": Doesn't say clearly that session replays and events are linked to the identified account (email and user id).
- "`[EU OR US REGION — CONFIRM]`": Doesn't disclose that the code switches region by token, so a US transfer depends on configuration.
- (absent) Mapbox's Analytics purpose is processing for Mapbox's own ends. The draft doesn't disclose it as such or offer any way to turn it off.

**3. LEGAL RISK**
- "contains no analytics or advertising software of any kind": Conflicts with the App Store privacy label. Apple requires third-party SDK collection to be declared, so the label must list User ID and Precise Location (not linked, App Functionality + Analytics). The policy and label would then contradict each other, and a "Data Not Collected" label would be a misrepresentation.
- "The app never asks for your device location": Users will see "Precise Location" on the label. The policy has to reconcile this explicitly instead of implying no location is collected.
- Precise geolocation (via Mapbox) is "sensitive personal information" under CPRA. It isn't identified as such, and limit-use rights are not addressed.
- "contact us and we will `[suppress analytics…]`": Opt-out by email is not valid for non-essential cookies and session replay under PECR / ePrivacy, which require prior consent. No consent banner exists.
- "This is deliberate: they are property and tenancy records…": "Deliberate" is not a lawful basis for keeping data after an erasure request (GDPR Art. 17(3)). A legal-obligation or legitimate-interest basis and a retention period are needed.
- "Delete your account … in the iPhone app": Apple guideline 5.1.1(v) requires the feature to be live at submission, and it isn't deployed.
- "We will respond within `[e.g. 30 days]`": GDPR allows one month, extendable by two; CCPA allows 45 days. A single fixed promise risks being wrong for one regime.
- "tell you in the product or by email before it takes effect": An unenforceable promise. No email provider is verified, and there's no defined notice period.
- "In the UK/EEA that is `[YOUR SUPERVISORY AUTHORITY]`": Singular is wrong. EEA residents can complain to the authority in their own member state.
- "No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will notify you…": Vague. Regulators expect the 72-hour authority-notification commitment and the "high risk" threshold for notifying individuals.
- (absent) The register and accept-invitation pages reference a privacy policy and terms, but every policy URL returns 404 in production. Users are agreeing to documents that don't exist, and the App Store requires a working privacy policy URL.
- "Identifiers … kept in a de-identified form": Misuses a regulated CCPA term (see section 1).

- DPO: no statement on whether one is required or appointed.

- Retention specifics:
