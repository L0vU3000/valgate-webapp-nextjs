## What we collect, why, and where it goes

### 1. Account and identity

| Data | Purpose | Processed by |
|---|---|---|
| Email address, display name, profile photo URL, user ID | Sign-in, account management, showing you who you are | Clerk (identity provider) |
| Workspace (organisation) name, your role in it (owner / admin / member / viewer) | Deciding what you may see and change | Clerk; our database |

We do not ask for your phone number, date of birth, government identifiers, or payment
details, and we do not store your password — authentication is handled by Clerk.

### 2. Records you enter about your properties

Property names, addresses and map coordinates, type and status, floor area, bedroom and
bathroom counts, year built, valuations, ownership records, and rental and tenancy
details. These are stored in our database (Neon, PostgreSQL) and are the substance of
the product.

### 3. Files you upload

Documents and photographs you attach to a property — deeds, leases, insurance
certificates and similar. Uploads go **directly from your device to object storage**
(Amazon Web Services S3) using a short-lived, single-purpose upload ticket issued by our
API; the file itself does not pass through, or sit on, our application servers. Files are
retrieved through short-lived signed URLs.

### 4. Security and service operation

Session tokens (stored in the iOS **keychain**, not in ordinary app storage), request
timestamps, IP address, and rate-limiting counters. We keep these to keep sessions valid,
prevent abuse, and diagnose faults.

### 5. Maps

The maps are provided by Mapbox. To render a map, Mapbox receives your device's request
and, per its own published privacy manifest, may collect a user ID and precise location,
for both app functionality and analytics. Mapbox declares that this data is **not used to
track you across other companies' apps or websites** (see "Tracking", below).

**The app never asks for your device location.** There is no location permission request
in the iPhone app; the maps are centred on the properties you have entered, not on where
you are.

### 6. Web analytics — web app only

The **web app** uses PostHog for product analytics and session replay, so that we can see
which parts of the product work and where people get stuck.

- Session replay is configured to **mask all on-screen text and all element attributes**,
  so property addresses, tenant names and lease figures are not captured in replays.
- Anonymous visitors are not given a profile. Only signed-in users are identified, so we
  can connect feedback to the account that gave it.
- This happens on the **web app only**. The iPhone app contains **no** analytics SDK, no
  crash-reporting SDK and no advertising SDK. Nothing you do in the app is sent to
  PostHog.

### 7. What we do not do

- We do not show advertising, and we have no advertising or data-broker SDK in either
  product.
- We do not sell your personal information.
- We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not use your
  data to build advertising profiles. Because we do not track, the iPhone app does not ask
  for App Tracking Transparency permission — there is nothing to permit.
